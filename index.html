Anyone who buys from GC can join. People move up based on how well their videos sell, not how many followers they have. The idea copies what worked for Myntra, Gymshark and Daniel Wellington.

How it runs

The same four steps repeat every month.

  1. Monthly brief

    We pick 2–3 products to push (people earn 1.5x on these) and share five easy video ideas.

    • The comfort test
    • One pant, three looks
    • Fit check by size
    • A day in my comfort zone
    • Colour haul

  2. Review

    We check every video within 48 hours. It must show the product clearly, say it's an ad, and make no claims we can't prove.

  3. Best videos become ads

    Every week, the best videos run as paid ads from the creator's account. What works goes into next month's brief.

  4. Tools

    A platform like Wishlink tracks links and pays creators. Wirality handles the reviews.

Staying legal in India

Free products, shopping credit and sales cuts all count as payment, so every video is an ad in the eyes of the rules.

RuleWhat it means for us
ASCI guidelinesEvery video must say it's an ad (#ad or Instagram's paid-partnership tag). We remove videos that don't.
Government endorsement rules
Fines up to ₹10 lakh, ₹50 lakh for repeats		The ad label must be in the video's language. Creators must have used the product. No claims we can't prove.
Tax on free products (Section 194R)If someone keeps more than ₹20,000 of free products or credit in a year, 10% tax must be deducted. We keep Levels 0–1 under this limit.
Tax on payments, and GSTTax is deducted before we pay. Creators registered for GST send a GST invoice. GC's CA should confirm the details under the new Income-tax Act (from April 2026).
Data and video rights (DPDP Act)Only people 18+. We ask clear permission before collecting PAN or UPI details, and written permission to use their videos in ads.
